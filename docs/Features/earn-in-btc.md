# Earn in Bitcoin

SkateHive pays its best skaters in **real Bitcoin**. Turn the HBD you earn from
your posts into on-chain BTC — sent straight to your own Bitcoin wallet — without
leaving SkateHive.

---

## ₿ What is it?

Your posts earn **HBD** (a stable dollar-pegged token on Hive). With Earn in
Bitcoin you can convert that HBD into **real BTC** on the Bitcoin network, powered
by [Magi](https://magi.eco) (a cross-chain DEX built on Hive/VSC).

It works **both ways**:

- **Sell → BTC** — turn your HBD (or HIVE) into real BTC.
- **Buy BTC →** — send real BTC and receive HBD/HIVE on your Hive account.

:::tip One signature, real Bitcoin
The whole thing is native to Hive — no bridges to babysit, no third-party
exchange account. You approve it in your Hive wallet and Magi settles BTC to your
address.
:::

---

## 🚀 Claim your rewards to BTC

The fastest path for creators:

1. Open your **Wallet** and find the **Rewards** card.
2. Tap **Claim to BTC** (or **Earn in BTC** if you're setting up ahead of time).
3. Follow the short checklist — the app guides you through anything missing.
4. Approve the signature. Magi converts your reward HBD and sends BTC to your
   address.

![The Earn in BTC / Claim to BTC button in the wallet Rewards card](/img/earn-in-btc/01-claim-to-btc-button.png)

You keep your Hive Power staked — only your **liquid HBD** is converted.

## ✅ What you need (and how to get it in-app)

Everything below can be set up **inside SkateHive** — the checklist offers a fix
for each item.

| Requirement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **A Bitcoin address** | Add it in **Settings → Bitcoin address** (or via the checklist). |
| **Magi Resource Credits (RC)** | Every account starts with enough base RC for a small swap. Need more? The app offers **Enable Magi RC** (see below). |
| **Liquid HBD to convert** | Claim your post rewards — the HBD portion is what converts. |

![The Claim to Bitcoin checklist — Bitcoin address, Magi RC, and reward](/img/earn-in-btc/02-checklist.png)

:::note
Converting to BTC needs your own Hive account with active-key access (e.g. Hive
Keychain). The **Buy BTC →** direction, on the other hand, only requires sending
BTC — no signature and no RC.
:::

## ⚡ Enable Magi RC

Swaps on Magi consume **Resource Credits (RC)** on the VSC network. This RC is
**not** from Hive Power — it comes from **HBD you keep parked in Magi/VSC**:

> **RC ceiling = 10,000 (base) + 1,000 × (HBD held in Magi)**

Two things to know:

- **Every account already has ~10,000 base RC**, which covers a small swap out of
  the box — most first swaps need no deposit at all.
- RC **recharges over time**. If yours is low (e.g. right after a swap), just wait
  a bit, or raise your ceiling by parking some HBD.

When RC is the blocker, the app shows an **Enable Magi RC** button. It deposits a
small amount of HBD into Magi to lift your RC ceiling. That HBD stays **your own
balance in Magi** — it's a refundable RC stake, not a fee.

:::tip Rule of thumb
Keep roughly as much HBD parked in Magi as the size of the swaps you want to do.
A swap of `X` HBD reserves about `X × 1000` RC, and the base 10k covers the rest.
:::

## 🔁 Buy BTC → HBD/HIVE

Want to go the other way and bring Bitcoin **into** Hive?

1. Open the **Bitcoin** tab in your wallet and switch to **BTC →**.
2. Choose what to receive: **HBD** or **HIVE**.
3. The app shows your **personal BTC deposit address** (with a QR code).
4. Send any amount of BTC to it. Magi converts it and settles HBD/HIVE to your
   Hive account after Bitcoin confirmations.

![The BTC → direction toggle and your personal BTC deposit address with QR code](/img/earn-in-btc/04-buy-btc-deposit.png)

No signature and no RC needed — it's just a Bitcoin send.

---

## 💸 Fees & good to know

- A small **1% referral fee** supports SkateHive on each conversion to BTC.
- The **Bitcoin network mining fee** is deducted from the BTC you receive (as with
  any on-chain withdrawal), so the amount that lands is slightly below the quote.
- Amounts are **real mainnet funds** — start small on your first swap.

:::warning Double-check your address
BTC transactions are irreversible. Make sure your Bitcoin address is correct
before converting.
:::
