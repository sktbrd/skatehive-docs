# Wallet Guide

The Skatehive wallet is your hub for managing all your crypto assets across multiple blockchains. Access it at [skatehive.app/wallet](https://skatehive.app/wallet).

---

## 🏦 Overview

![The wallet overview with aggregated balances across Hive, EVM and Farcaster](/img/wallet/01-overview.png)

The wallet has four tabs — **Wallet Overview · Art · SkateBank · PIX** — and the
overview aggregates your balances across **Hive, EVM and Farcaster** into one
portfolio (filter with the All Chains / Hive / EVM / Farcaster pills):

- **Wallet Overview** — total value, every token across chains, send/receive, swap.
- **Art** — your NFTs.
- **SkateBank** — HBD savings (15% APR) and savings jars.
- **PIX** — Brazilian fiat (BRL) transfers via PIX.

You can also convert your rewards into **real Bitcoin** — see
[Earn in Bitcoin](./earn-in-btc).

## 💰 Hive Tokens

### HIVE

Your liquid HIVE tokens that can be:
- **Transferred** to other users via the Send button
- **Powered up** to Hive Power for increased influence
- **Traded** on exchanges

### Hive Power (HP)

Staked HIVE that gives you:
- **Voting power**: More HP = stronger votes on content
- **Curation rewards**: Earn by upvoting quality content
- **Resource credits**: Needed to post, vote, and transact on Hive
- **Governance**: Vote for witnesses who run the blockchain

> 💡 **Power Up**: Click "Power Up" to convert HIVE to HP. Power Down takes 13 weeks to convert back in weekly payments.

### Hive Backed Dollars (HBD)

A stablecoin pegged to ~$1 USD:
- **Stable value**: Great for savings
- **15% APR**: Earn interest by putting HBD in SkateBank savings
- **Send**: Transfer HBD to other users with optional memo

## 🏦 SkateBank (HBD Savings)

![The SkateBank savings tab](/img/wallet/02-skatebank.png)

SkateBank is Skatehive's savings section where you earn **15% APR** on your HBD:

### How It Works

1. Go to [skatehive.app/wallet](https://skatehive.app/wallet)
2. Navigate to the **SkateBank** tab
3. You'll see:
   - Your current HBD Savings balance
   - Available liquid HBD to invest
   - Estimated claimable interest
   - Days until next interest claim

### Key Features

- **15% Annual Interest**: Guaranteed returns on your HBD savings
- **Monthly Interest Claims**: Claim your earned interest
- **3-Day Withdrawal Period**: Savings withdrawals take 3 days for security
- **Pending Withdrawals**: See any active withdrawal requests

### Actions

- **Add to Savings**: Move liquid HBD into savings to start earning
- **Withdraw from Savings**: Initiate a 3-day withdrawal back to liquid HBD

## 🔄 Swap

Swap right from the wallet, across chains:

- **Hive** — HIVE ↔ HBD conversions.
- **Bitcoin** — HBD/HIVE → real BTC, and BTC → HBD/HIVE, powered by Magi. See
  [Earn in Bitcoin](./earn-in-btc).
- **ERC-20** — cross-chain token swaps sourced from 150+ liquidity sources for the
  best price (on Ethereum, Base and more).

## 🐷 Savings Jars

Inside SkateBank you can create **savings jars (cofrinhos)** — goal-based buckets
to set money aside for something specific (a new deck, a trip, an event). Allocate
HBD to a jar and track progress toward your goal.

## 🇧🇷 PIX

Brazilian skaters can move between crypto and **BRL via PIX** directly in the
wallet. The **PIX** tab includes a transfer guide, an FAQ, and your PIX
transaction history.

## 📤 Send Tokens

Transfer tokens to other users:

1. Click **"Send"** on HIVE or HBD
2. Enter the **recipient's Hive username**
3. Enter the **amount**
4. Add a **memo** (optional, but required for exchanges)
5. Confirm with Hive Keychain or your login method

> ⚠️ **Double-check**: Blockchain transactions are irreversible. Always verify the recipient username.

## 🔗 Connecting Wallets

### Hive Wallet
Connected when you log in with:
- Hive Keychain
- Ecency
- Or other Aioha-supported methods

### Ethereum Wallet
1. Click **"Connect Wallet"**
2. Choose your wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, Coinbase Wallet)
3. Approve the connection
4. Your assets appear automatically
