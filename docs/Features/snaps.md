# Snaps

**Snaps** are SkateHive's short-form feed — quick posts to share clips, photos and
thoughts with the community. Think of them as skate-native microblogging, posted
straight to the Hive blockchain.

---

## 🛹 The feed

The home page is the Snaps feed: a live stream of what the community is posting.
Scroll, watch clips, upvote, and jump into the replies.

![The Snaps feed on the home page](/img/snaps/01-feed.png)

---

## ✍️ Posting a Snap

Use the composer at the top of the feed:

![The Snap composer](/img/snaps/02-composer.png)

You can add:

- **Text** — your caption or thought.
- **Media** — photos and videos. Videos are uploaded, and you can trim them and
  pick a thumbnail frame.
- **GIFs** — from Giphy, or make your own with the built-in GIF maker.

Hit post and your Snap goes on-chain (or via your SkateHive account if you're a
[lite account](../Get%20Started/accounts-and-identities)).

:::tip Cross-post while you're at it
When you post a Snap you can also share it to **Instagram** and **Farcaster** in
the same flow — see [Cross-posting](./cross-posting).
:::

---

## 👍 Voting & replies

- **Upvote** Snaps you like — use the vote-weight slider to control how much of
  your voting power you give.
- **Reply** to join the conversation; replies are threaded.

Your upvotes help distribute rewards to skaters.

---

## 💰 Snaps earn rewards

Snaps earn **HBD** rewards from upvotes over a 7-day window. You can see your
"Snaps potential" in the wallet, and turn those rewards into real Bitcoin — see
[Earn in Bitcoin](./earn-in-btc).
