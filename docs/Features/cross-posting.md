# Cross-posting

Post once on SkateHive, share everywhere. When you publish a [Snap](./snaps), you
can also send it to **Farcaster** and **Instagram** — without leaving the app.

---

## 🟣 Farcaster

Link your Farcaster identity (in **Settings → App Account**) and approve a signer
once. After that, when you post a Snap you can choose where it goes:

- **Hive** only
- **Farcaster** only
- **Both**

Your Snap is cast to Farcaster with the right text and media embeds, and replies
link back to the related post so conversations stay connected.

:::note
Farcaster cross-posting needs a linked Farcaster account with an approved signer.
Set it up once and it just works from then on.
:::

---

## 📸 Instagram

Selected Snaps can be cross-posted to the **SkateHive Instagram** to give skaters
reach beyond the app. Before it publishes, a review dialog lets you:

- **Edit the caption** (legenda).
- **Add collaborators** (Instagram Collab), so the post appears on their profiles
  too.
- Toggle the **SkateHive logo overlay**.

The app also suggests smart caption ideas when it detects tricks or spots in your
post.

---

## 👀 Unified preview

Before anything goes out, a single **preview dialog** shows how your post will look
on **Hive, Instagram and Farcaster** side by side — so you can confirm everything
in one place.

The cross-post options live right in the [Snap composer](./snaps) — the media, GIF
and Instagram icons below the text box:

![The Snap composer, where cross-posting starts](/img/snaps/02-composer.png)

Only snaps are cross-posted to Instagram; long-form magazine posts with multiple
media can be published as an Instagram **carousel**.
