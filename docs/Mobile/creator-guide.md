# Creator Guide

Everything you need to post your first clip and grow on Skatehive — from recording to rewards.

---

## Step 1: Record your clip

You have two options:

**Option A — Record directly in the app**
1. Tap the **+** tab
2. Select **Video**
3. Tap the record button — hold for as long as you need
4. Tap stop when done
5. Preview and proceed to upload

**Option B — Use your phone's camera**
Record with your native camera app for more control (slow motion, higher resolution, manual focus). Then import it from your gallery when creating a post.

**Tips for better clips:**
- Shoot horizontal (landscape) — vertical clips are cropped in the feed
- Good light makes a bigger difference than the camera
- Multiple angles of the same trick give you options to pick the best one
- Clean up your background when possible — a busy background distracts from the skating

---

## Step 2: Create your post

Tap the **+** tab and choose the format:

| Format | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Snap** | Single clip, quick update, no long description needed |
| **Article** | Full skate edit, trip report, multi-clip video with context |

For a raw clip: **Snap** is usually the right call.  
For a full edit or something you want to write about: **Article**.

---

## Step 3: Upload your video

1. Select your clip from the gallery (or use what you just recorded)
2. Add a **title** — keep it short and descriptive
3. Add **tags** — always include `#skatehive` first, then add trick names, location, or style tags (e.g. `#kickflip`, `#barcelona`, `#street`)
4. Tap **Upload**

The app sends your video to a transcoding server that converts it to web-optimized H.264 and pins it to IPFS. This happens in the background — you'll see a progress indicator.

**Do not close the app while uploading.** Stay on WiFi for videos over 50MB.

---

## Step 4: Wait for processing

Processing time depends on file size and server load:

| Clip length | Typical wait |
|---|---|
| Under 30 seconds | 1–3 min |
| 30s – 2 min | 3–10 min |
| Over 2 min | 10–30 min |

If processing stalls for more than 30 minutes, force-close the app and try again. The system has three servers in failover — if the primary is overloaded, it automatically switches to a backup.

---

## Step 5: Publish

Once processing is done, tap **Publish**. Your post is written to the HIVE blockchain — it's permanent and publicly accessible within 30–60 seconds.

You'll see it in your profile immediately. It appears in the community feed once propagated across HIVE nodes.

---

## Step 6: Engage to grow

The Skatehive leaderboard and reward system favor active community members — not just post frequency.

**What actually moves the needle:**
- **Vote on others' content** — curators earn a share of the post's rewards. Voting also signals who you support.
- **Leave real comments** — short, genuine comments get noticed. Generic ones don't.
- **Reply to comments on your posts** — the algorithm and community both reward engagement.
- **Post consistently** — one solid clip per week beats a burst of ten followed by silence.
- **Use relevant tags** — `#skatehive` gets you into the main feed. Trick and location tags help people find you.

---

## Understanding your rewards

Every post that receives upvotes accumulates rewards over 7 days. At payout you receive:

- **HP (HIVE Power)** — staked HIVE that increases your voting influence over time
- **HBD (Hive Backed Dollars)** — liquid stable token (~$1 USD each)

The more HP you accumulate, the more your own votes are worth — which means more curation rewards when you upvote others early.

To check balances and manage funds, use the [web app](https://skatehive.app) — full wallet features are not available in the mobile app due to App Store restrictions.

---

## Checklist for every post

- [ ] Clip is horizontal (landscape)
- [ ] Title is clear and describes what's happening
- [ ] `#skatehive` is the first tag
- [ ] At least 2–3 more relevant tags added
- [ ] On WiFi if the file is large
- [ ] App stays open during upload

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

**Posting without `#skatehive`** — your post won't appear in the community feed. Always include it.

**Closing the app mid-upload** — interrupts the transfer. The video may fail to process and you'll have to start over.

**Only posting, never voting** — rewards and growth on HIVE are social. Voting on others' content is how you participate in the ecosystem and earn curation rewards.

**Posting the same clip multiple times** — HIVE content is permanent. If a post didn't get traction, don't repost — engage more with the community first and try again with the next clip.
