# Features

### What is the Leaderboard?

The **Leaderboard** tab ranks community members by activity and engagement. It shows top posters, most upvoted content, and most active contributors. It resets periodically to give everyone a fair chance.

---

### How do notifications work?

The **Notifications** tab shows activity on your posts and profile: upvotes, comments, follows, and mentions. Push notifications are currently in development — for now, check the tab manually to see new activity.

---

### Can I follow other users?

Yes. Visit any user's profile and tap **Follow**. Their posts will appear in your feed. You can manage who you follow from **Profile → Following**.

---

### Some features I've seen on the website aren't in the app. Why?

Several features are only available on the [Skatehive web app](https://skatehive.app) — the mobile app focuses on core social features. Some features like the Spot Map are accessible via the browser on mobile but not yet in the native app. See [Mobile vs Web App](../app-vs-web.md) for a full comparison.
