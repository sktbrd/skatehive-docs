# Wallet & Rewards

### How do I earn HIVE rewards?

Every post and comment you publish can receive upvotes from other users. Upvotes translate into HIVE rewards, split between **Author Rewards** (you) and **Curation Rewards** (the voter). Rewards are paid out 7 days after posting.

---

### When do I receive my earnings?

Rewards are released **7 days after the post's publication date**. After payout, they are split into:

- **HP (HIVE Power)** — staked HIVE that increases your voting influence
- **HBD (Hive Backed Dollars)** — stable token pegged to ~$1 USD

Check your earnings on the [Skatehive web app](https://skatehive.app) — full wallet management (send, swap, stake) is only available there. The mobile app intentionally limits wallet features to comply with App Store guidelines, which restrict in-app cryptocurrency transactions.

---

### How does voting power work?

Each upvote you cast uses a portion of your **Voting Power**, which regenerates at ~20% per day. Casting many votes quickly drains it, reducing the weight of each vote. The app shows your current voting power in your profile.

---

### What is HIVE Power (HP)?

HIVE Power is staked HIVE. The more HP you hold, the more your upvote is worth and the more curation rewards you earn. HP cannot be transferred instantly — it must be **powered down** over 13 weeks.
