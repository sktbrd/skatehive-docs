# Troubleshooting

### The app crashes on startup

1. Force-close the app and reopen it
2. Check for an update in the App Store
3. Restart your device
4. If it still crashes, uninstall and reinstall the app (your keys are stored in the secure keychain — they survive reinstallation on most devices)
5. Report the issue in [Discord #bugs](https://discord.gg/skatehive) with your device model and OS version

---

### I can't log in — what should I check?

- Make sure you're entering your **Posting Key**, not your master password or active key
- Check that your HIVE username is spelled correctly (case-insensitive, but no spaces)
- Verify your internet connection
- Try clearing the app cache (Android: Settings → Apps → Skatehive → Clear Cache)
- If you recently changed your posting key on another device, the saved key in the app is outdated — log out and log in again with the new key

---

### The feed is not loading

- Pull down to refresh
- Check your internet connection
- The HIVE node may be temporarily overloaded — wait 1–2 minutes and try again
- Force-close and reopen the app

---

### My post isn't appearing in the feed

- It can take 30–60 seconds for a new post to propagate across HIVE nodes
- Make sure you included the `#skatehive` tag
- Check your profile directly to confirm the post was published

---

### How do I report a bug or request a feature?

- **Discord:** [#bugs channel](https://discord.gg/skatehive)
- **GitHub:** [Open an issue](https://github.com/SkateHive/mobileapp/issues)

Include your device model, OS version, and a description of what happened.

For more detailed troubleshooting, see the [full Troubleshooting guide](../troubleshooting.md).
