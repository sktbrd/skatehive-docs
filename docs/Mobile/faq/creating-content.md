# Creating Content

### How do I create a post?

Tap the **+** (Create) tab at the bottom of the screen. You can choose between:

- **Article** — long-form post with Markdown support, ideal for full write-ups with images
- **Snap** — quick short post for updates, clips, or photos

Fill in the title, content, and tags, then tap **Publish**.

---

### What is the difference between an Article and a Snap?

| | Article | Snap |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Long-form | Short / quick update |
| Formatting | Full Markdown | Basic text |
| Best for | Tutorials, trip reports | Quick clips, updates |
| Tags | Multiple | Fewer |

Both are stored on the HIVE blockchain and can earn rewards.

---

### What tags should I use?

Always include **`#skatehive`** as a tag — it ensures your post appears in the Skatehive feed. Add other relevant tags like `#skating`, `#streetskating`, or location tags to reach a wider audience.

---

### Can I edit a post after publishing?

Yes, with limitations. HIVE allows editing posts within **7 days** of publishing. After the payout window closes, edits are still recorded on-chain but the original version is also preserved. Tap the three-dot menu on your post → **Edit**.
