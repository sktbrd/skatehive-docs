# Getting Started

### Where can I download the app?

- **iOS:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/skatehive/id6738086962)
- **Android:** Google Play (coming soon)

---

### Do I need a HIVE account to use the app?

Yes, to post, vote, comment, or earn rewards you need a HIVE account. You can browse the feed and watch videos in **Spectator Mode** without logging in.

You can create a HIVE account in two ways:
- **With email** — sign up directly inside the app. Quick and doesn't require any crypto knowledge.
- **Via wallet.hive.io** — for users who want full control from the start.

[Create a HIVE account →](../../Get%20Started/create-account.md)

---

### Can I try the app before creating an account?

Yes. Open the app and tap **Continue as Guest** (Spectator Mode). You can browse the feed, watch videos, and explore profiles — no account required. You'll need to log in to interact with content.
