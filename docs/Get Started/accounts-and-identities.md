# Accounts & Identities

SkateHive is built on one idea: **the way you sign up shouldn't limit what you can
do.** However you join, you can post, vote and comment right away — and connect
more identities over time.

---

## 🚪 Four ways in — all equal

You can join SkateHive through any of these, and each gets full functionality:

| Entry point | You sign in with |
|---|---|
| **Email** | A magic link — no password. |
| **EVM wallet** | MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc. |
| **Farcaster** | Sign in with Farcaster. |
| **Hive Keychain** | Direct Hive blockchain login (Keychain, HiveAuth, PeakVault). |

No matter which you pick, you get a SkateHive profile and can **post, vote and
comment immediately.**

---

## 🪶 Lite vs Full accounts

The only difference is *where your posts live on-chain*:

- **Lite account** — you haven't linked a Hive key yet (e.g. email / wallet /
  Farcaster-only). Your posts and votes are broadcast for you through a shared
  SkateHive account, but the app shows **your** name, avatar and profile
  everywhere. You don't see any of the blockchain plumbing.
- **Full account** — you have your own Hive key (via Keychain or a stored posting
  key). Posts go on-chain **under your own Hive username** with full ownership.

You never get stuck as "lite" — you can upgrade any time, and lite accounts keep
working forever.

---

## ⬆️ Upgrade paths (Lite → Full)

1. **Get sponsored** — any established skater can sponsor you a real Hive account.
   Your posting key is created and stored securely, and you start posting under
   your own name. See [Account Sponsorship](./sponsorship).
2. **Link an existing Hive account** — already have Hive from elsewhere? Link it
   with a Keychain signature and post directly.
3. **Store your posting key** — have a Hive account and want convenience? Save your
   posting key so you can log in with email/wallet **and** post without a Keychain
   popup every time.

---

## 🔗 Connect more identities

One SkateHive account can hold **all** your identities — Hive, EVM wallet(s) and
Farcaster — so you log in with any of them and keep the same profile.

Manage this in **Settings → App Account**:

![Linking identities and account settings](/img/accounts/02-app-account-settings.png)

- **Link** an EVM wallet, Farcaster, or Hive account to your profile.
- **Merge** two accounts if you accidentally created separate ones (e.g. signed up
  with email months ago, then with a wallet today) — everything is combined.
- Manage your **stored posting key**, newsletter, and Bitcoin address here too.

:::tip
Linking a Hive identity is what unlocks posting under your own username. EVM and
Farcaster links add wallet and social features on top.
:::

---

## 🤝 Merging accounts

If you try to link an identity that's already attached to another SkateHive
account, we'll offer to **merge** them. Merging preserves everything — all your
linked identities, profile data and history are combined into one account. Nothing
is lost.
