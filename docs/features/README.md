# Features

Everything SkateHive can do, in one place. Browse the guides for each feature:

- **[Wallet](./wallet)** — your multi-chain wallet: Hive, EVM and Farcaster
  balances, send/receive, swaps, savings, PIX and more.
- **[Earn in Bitcoin](./earn-in-btc)** — turn your HBD rewards into real BTC.
- **[Snaps](./snaps)** — the short-form skate feed and composer.
- **[Cross-posting](./cross-posting)** — share your Snaps to Instagram and
  Farcaster.
- **[Spot Map](./spot-map)** — find and submit skate spots.
- **[Leaderboard](./leaderboard)** — how skaters are ranked.
- **[Bounties](./bounties)** — trick challenges with crypto rewards.
- **[Video Upload](./video-upload)** — share your clips.
- **[DAO & Governance](./dao-governance)** — community treasury and voting.
